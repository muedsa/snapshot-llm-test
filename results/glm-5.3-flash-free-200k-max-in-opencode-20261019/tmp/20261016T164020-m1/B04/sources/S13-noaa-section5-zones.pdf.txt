The upper 200 m of the ocean is termed the photic zone. Sunlight penetrates sufficiently to support the growth of phytoplankton and/or macro algae.

The area between 200 m and 1,000 m is also called the "twilight" zone in popular sources. Light intensity is severely reduced with increasing depth so light penetration is minimal.

Sunlight does not penetrate this eternal darkness. Popular sources sometimes refer to this as the midnight zone. About 5% of the primary production from the surface makes it to the bottom. Less food means lower biomass.
