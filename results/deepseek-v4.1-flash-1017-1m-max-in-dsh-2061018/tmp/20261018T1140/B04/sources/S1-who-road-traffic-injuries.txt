Key facts: ~1.16 million deaths/year; leading cause of death ages 5-29; 92% fatalities in low/middle-income countries with ~60% of vehicles; more than half of deaths among pedestrians, cyclists, motorcyclists; cost most countries 3% GDP; 20-50 million non-fatal injuries; males ~3x; 2/3 deaths working age 18-59; speed +1% mean -> +4% fatal crash risk; pedestrian death risk 4.5x from 50 to 65 km/h; helmet >6x death reduction / up to 74% brain injury; seatbelt up to 50%; child restraint 71% infant deaths; phone use ~4x crash; BAC >=0.04 g/dl significant; UN target halve by 2030.
