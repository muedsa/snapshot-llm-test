# case-09 · The health ledger

**Reader question this work answers**: What is the treaty credited with preventing?

- **Final render**: `final.png`, raw bytes of a 200 `image/png` response, from `tmp/20261003-114508-flashmax/B04/renders/case-09.v3.png`
- **DSL**: `final.snapshot` — version `v3`, complete and self-contained, no embedded images
- **Structure**: ledger: three modelled health totals, then cost against benefit
- **Reported size**: 1600×1150 px
- **Data basis**: US EPA modelling as reported by the Secretariat: 443 M skin-cancer cases, 2.3 M deaths, 63 M cataracts; US$5.1 bn fund; US$1.8 tn and US$460 bn benefits
- **Illustrative or modelled elements**: every figure is a model result and the sheet says so in its own panel
- **Sources cited on the sheet**: S4 (full records in `../sources.json`)
- **Visual review**: v1 let the benefit labels run off the right edge and left half the canvas empty; v2 shortened them; v3 narrowed the bars so the labels fit, and added the note that the cost bar is 2 px long at this scale
- **Rejected attempts kept**: none
- **Fictional content**: the only invented elements are the special's masthead name, issue number and editor byline, both declared in `../editorial-note.md`. No data, quote or figure on this sheet is invented: every number is either transcribed from the cited source or computed from it in `scripts/ozone.py`, and derived quantities are labelled as derived.
- **Supporting assets**: none. The artwork is pure DSL.
